**Un-tie by Sound Brewery** splits mixed dialogue into one stem per character.

Download the **apple-silicon** .dmg for M1/M2/M3/M4 Macs or the **intel** .dmg for Intel Macs, then follow "READ ME FIRST" inside it.

- Finds the characters automatically (or from voice samples you mark)
- Lines spoken alone go to the character's stem untouched; only overlaps are separated
- Full-bandwidth overlaps rebuilt from your original recording
- Stems + residual null against the original
- Timeline fixes, per-stem listening, BWF timecode kept, 24-bit or 32-bit float export
- Pro Tools AAF export: all stems as tracks at the source timecode, with an optional guide track of the original mix

**1.1.0**
- Three people talking at once: a dedicated three-voice separator for those stretches
- Splitting intensity slider (Natural to Strict)
- Voice analysis report per character (in the app and saved with every export)
